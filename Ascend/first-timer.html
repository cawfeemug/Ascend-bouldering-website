Waiver & Registration

Before climbing, everyone must fill in a waiver. You can do this online or in-person. Make sure you bring valid ID for verification at the front desk.

Gear Rental & Safety

Need gear? We've got you covered. Our staff will guide you through gear fitting, basic safety rules, and how to fall safely on the mats.

Climb Time!

Start exploring the walls at your own pace. There are routes for every level — just ask our friendly staff if you're unsure where to begin.

Gym Facilities

Train hard, climb stronger. Our gym is fully equipped with free weights, barbells, and cable machines — perfect for strength training and conditioning to support your climbing goals.

Have a Break

Need a quick refuel? Our in-house café serves freshly brewed coffee and a variety of light bites — perfect for a post-climb recharge or a cozy catch-up between sessions.

First Ascent

No experience? No problem! Try out our introductory class led by our friendly and experienced instructors — giving you the confidence and fundamentals you need to get started.
